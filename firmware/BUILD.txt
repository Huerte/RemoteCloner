Prebuilt firmware for IRRemoteCloner.

Files are named <role>_<chip>.hex
  receiver_*  captures IR signals (sketch: arduino/ir_receiver/ir_receiver.ino, IR receiver output on D11)
  sender_*    transmits IR signals (sketch: arduino/ir_sender/ir_sender.ino, IR LED on D3)
  *_atmega328p.hex  Arduino Uno and Nano, raw buffer 200
  *_atmega2560.hex  Arduino Mega 2560, raw buffer 750

Built with avr-gcc 7.3.0, Arduino AVR core 1.8.6 and IRremote 4.7.1 (commit c4ec014), F_CPU 16 MHz.
The hex files are installed by the Set up board page in the app, which runs avrdude.
